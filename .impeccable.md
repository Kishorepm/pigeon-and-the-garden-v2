## Design Context

### Users
Two partners on their phones and computers, sharing date memories, missions, moods, notes and food ratings in a private virtual kingdom. iPhone Safari is a primary target.

### Brand Personality
Playful, intimate, unhurried. A small medieval world filled with personal callbacks, pets and places to return to.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved overhead LPC pixel-art scenery and layered characters. Green grounds, textured cottages, a shared castle and parchment journals. The user explicitly prefers the original game feeling over a busy redesign. North is his home; South is hers. Keep personal details gentle, including Pearl's memorial.

### Design Principles
1. Make the world playable: direct movement, understandable paths, responsive controls and discoveries near the character.
2. Extend the existing visual language rather than replacing it with a dashboard or extra decoration.
3. Give phones full functionality with generous touch targets and a clear view of the character.
4. Keep motion readable; respect reduced motion and pause movement when reading or typing.
5. Make shared versus on-device preview data explicit. Never imply an unconnected backend is live.
