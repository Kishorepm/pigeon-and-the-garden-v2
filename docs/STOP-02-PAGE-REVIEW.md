# Stop 02: page review and proposed next chapter

Reviewed 8 September 2026 against the handover, source and all 23 local screen previews. The opening curtain is an additional overlay. This is the historical review preceding the illustrated experiment. The user subsequently chose a return to the original pixel style; the active implementation is documented in PIXEL-SEQUEL.md.

## The central finding

Stop 02 currently begins by introducing the petitioner again, asking for another yes, celebrating the answer, choosing company and explaining an outfit correction. The actual continuation map arrives only after that. This makes a returning visitor restart the relationship's introduction instead of stepping into its next chapter.

The first refresh improved legibility and choice layouts, but the world is still a backdrop. A high-production version needs the environment to carry the story: the camera advances, the route changes, choices alter the destination, and the final scene visibly reflects the invitation.

## Proposed route: ten scenes, with optional details

1. **The road continues.** A stamped 01 marker, returning pigeon, and the new 02 route. One opening action.
2. **The unfinished invitation.** A short meal note and the single amended term. Continue or leave.
3. **The garden fork.** Choose the setting; the world acknowledges the choice.
4. **Make a little time.** Day and time-of-day together; duration can be adjusted here.
5. **This time, food.** Choose sweet, savoury or both. The iced drink is already there.
6. **A new stretch of path.** A short camera movement across a bridge or through a garden gate. Skippable; not another mandatory question.
7. **A table, at last.** The destination is revealed, with the unfinished castle beyond it. The tortoise is an optional discovery.
8. **One finishing touch.** Optional crown placement on the existing throne. Company and inherited details remain editable without redoing the route.
9. **The invitation, made yours.** A legible summary with direct editing and a seal.
10. **Stop 02 is stamped.** Pigeon departure, lit table, final plan and the quiet unopened road onward. One ending.

The decline state is outside this main route. Exact scene count may reduce further if the camera movement and arrival work better as one sequence.

## Visual direction for approval

**Recommended: A, the lantern garden.** A richly illustrated garden framed by foliage and a stone arch. Warm evening light, deep green foliage, readable ivory type, and a clear path from the completed marker to the new destination. The generated comparison board also contains B, a denser cinematic pixel-art interpretation that preserves more of the first version's game language.

Physical context: a returning recipient opens the link on a phone, perhaps after work, and should immediately recognise the garden while feeling that a new chapter has begun.

The core UI will remain actual text and controls. The comparison image is an art-direction probe, not a flattened page to ship. Build separate illustrated scenery, pigeon and foreground assets so motion can create depth. Preserve the pigeon, road, garden, green outfit, iced drink and existing throne as recognisable continuity motifs. The newly permitted style change supersedes the old fixed pixel-grid and CSS-only-motion restrictions.

## Motion and implementation

Use a pinned, locally served copy of **Anime.js**, with its licence included. Its timeline and SVG path APIs fit the camera choreography, messenger flight and route reveal. This is a proposed dependency, not yet installed.

- Signature moment: the sealed invitation triggers a stamp, a lantern sequence and the pigeon's flight along the new route.
- Scene continuity: three independently moving visual layers, rather than fading between unrelated cards.
- Choice feedback: brief visual acknowledgement before moving on; the selected choice remains visible when returning.
- Ambient motion: sparse foliage movement, pigeon idles and lantern light; pause when the page is hidden.
- Reduced motion: settled illustrations and immediate navigation, without forced camera travel.
- Mobile: generous controls, no inaccessible content below a fixed viewport, and a usable fallback when imagery or animation fails.
- Keep the existing response endpoint contract and keep all automated testing in notification-disabled previews.

Official sources: [Anime.js timelines](https://animejs.com/documentation/timeline/), [motion paths](https://animejs.com/documentation/svg/createmotionpath/), [licence](https://github.com/juliangarnier/anime/blob/master/LICENSE.md).

## Build acceptance criteria

- The first scene visibly continues from completed Stop 01 into a new Stop 02 route.
- No repeated petitioner, mandatory outfit, mandatory iced-drink, or repeated throne selection screens.
- Every essential date choice reaches the summary and response payload correctly.
- Back, direct editing, decline and repeat sealing work without duplicate accidental submission.
- The arrival reflects the selected setting and food.
- The live result preserves the approved concept's composition and visual richness.
- Verify 320×568, 360×640, 390×844, larger phones and desktop framing; test reduced motion, keyboard focus, loading failure and rapid repeated taps.
