# The Pigeon and the Garden

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

A returning invitation recipient opens a private, playful garden journey on a portrait phone. The experience lets them choose the shape of a second outing, then seal a decree. The established Stop 02 handover and repository README supply this context; no recipient biography belongs in design assets.

## Personality

Cosy, handmade, dryly funny. A storybook farm garden with a pigeon, a winding road and an unfinished castle. The experience itself is the creative artifact.

The approved sequel uses the original clear pixel game, not the subsequently rejected illustrated storybook experiment. Stop 01 leads to a new food-and-iced-drinks outing, travel choices that offer to come to her side, and the future crown reward. The selected throne is inherited. The central castle stays unfinished; the crown appears only after the actual second date. Pets already live at home. The completed castle and dragon are the longer story arc, not the end of Stop 02.

## Anti-references

No conventional romance symbols, neon, glitch, CRT effects, generic questionnaire styling, stock photography or glossy app surfaces. Preserve the existing pixel-art palette and locally vendored type.

## Principles

Make choices feel like encounters in a garden. Use varied compositions while keeping one obvious action per beat. Preserve the returning-stop narrative and existing response behavior. Keep the final decree readable and give it a deliberate visual payoff.

## Accessibility

One scene at a time, portrait mobile first, safe-area clearance, minimum 44px controls, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support. Fit the ordinary portrait flow without scrolling; allow accessible overflow on unusually small screens and a scrolling two-column landscape layout. Use system type for longer reading. No sound, analytics, accounts or third-party asset requests.
